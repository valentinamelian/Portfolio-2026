My process

Every project starts with listening. Then comes research, strategy, and execution. This is what makes the difference between content that fills a feed and content that actually works for a brand.

01
Brief & Research
I study the brand, its audience, competitors, and current digital presence before touching anything.
02
Strategy
Objectives, brand voice, content pillars, target audience — a roadmap before a single post goes live.
03
Creation
Copy, design, video — produced in-house and adapted to each platform's format and algorithm.
04
Publish & Iterate
Scheduling, monitoring, and data-informed adjustments. The work doesn't end at publish.
